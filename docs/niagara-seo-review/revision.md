## Questions

### Can you work on SEO without rebuilding my website?

Yes, where the platform and access support the changes. We review the current site before deciding what needs to change. A defined search project can be scoped separately from the monthly website and growth packages.

### How much does Niagara SEO cost?

Standalone SEO work is quoted for the agreed scope. If you also need a website and ongoing marketing, the packages above start at $199 CAD per month plus $750 setup. That is the Launch website and marketing package, not a fixed price for standalone SEO.

### How will we assess progress?

We agree on the measures before work begins. Where data is available, we review which searches show your pages, which pages people visit, and what they enquire about. More visibility is useful, but it does not by itself establish more enquiries or sales.

### How long does local SEO take?

The timeline depends on your site and the work we agree on. Completing a page or technical fix does not mean search results change immediately. We set a work schedule and review the available search data over time.

### Do you guarantee rankings or AI recommendations?

No. Google, ChatGPT, and Claude choose their own results. We agree on the work and how progress will be assessed without promising rankings, citations, or sales.
