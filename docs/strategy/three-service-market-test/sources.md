## Collected keyword data

`data/keywords-canada.json` and `data/keywords-usa.json` are successful raw responses from DataForSEO's Google Ads search-volume endpoint through Treg. `data/keyword-sample.csv` is the flattened comparison.

Collected September 25, 2026; requested period September 2025 through August 2026; English; country codes 2124 and 2840; search partners excluded. Nine identical automation-related phrases in each country. Two calls at US$0.09 each, total reported cost US$0.18.

Use the provider's reported volume as an estimate. Unavailable values are not zero. Searchers include non-buyers. Paid-search competition does not measure consulting competition or SEO difficulty. Do not add overlapping queries as unique buyers or infer sales conversion. The sample contains no website or growth-marketing terms.

[Endpoint definitions](https://docs.dataforseo.com/v3/keywords_data/google_ads/search_volume/live/).
