## Help couples see what you offer, what it costs, and how to book a tour.

The venue is usually the first vendor couples book: 82% of couples hired their venue before anyone else.¹ They compare venues online first, so your site should answer their first questions clearly: how much it costs, how many guests fit, and how to see the space. I rebuild venue websites around those questions, so each enquiry arrives with the date, guest count, and budget already filled in.

### What the rebuild includes

- **Pricing guide:** starting prices or packages, shown the way you want couples to see them
- **Spaces and capacity:** each space with guest counts, layouts, and photos
- **Packages and inclusions**, written in plain language
- **Answers to common questions:** catering rules, rain plan, lodging, preferred vendors
- **Galleries** built from your photographers' images [confirm photo permissions]
- **An enquiry form** that asks for the date, guest count, and budget
- **A link to book a tour** through your current calendar or booking tool
- **Google Business Profile** updated to match the site
- **Clear, consistent venue details** that Google, ChatGPT, and Claude can read
- **Mobile checks** of every page and form
