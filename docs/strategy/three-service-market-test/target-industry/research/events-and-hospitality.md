# Vertical research: events and hospitality (researched 2026-09-26)

Industries: (1) independent wedding and event venues; (2) wineries, cideries, craft distilleries; (3) event performers and entertainers.

Labels: [A] = assumption or my calculation; [NE] = no evidence found; [LQ] = low-quality source (vendor or agency marketing, blog, or undisclosed method). "Accessed" dates are 2026-09-26 unless stated. Business names and URLs from the website sample are in the separate private file only.

Currency: USD unless marked CAD.

## 1. Wedding and event venues (independent, owner-run)

### A. Size

- **No clean NAICS code.** Venues are split across NAICS 531120 (lessors of non-residential buildings, which includes halls rented without catering), 722320 (caterers, which includes banquet halls with catering), 711310/711311 (presenters with facilities), and farm operations classed in agriculture [A]. Counts below are proxies, not venue counts.
- **Canada, StatCan Canadian Business Counts, December 2025** (tables 33-10-1095-01 with employees and 33-10-1096-01 without employees, released 2026-02-13; queried via the StatCan WDS API): 722320 caterers 2,478 employer locations plus 3,679 without employees; Ontario 957 plus 1,668. 531120 lessors of non-residential buildings: 9,882 plus 84,014 (far too broad to use). https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/t1/tbl1/en/tv.action?pid=3310109501 and https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/t1/tbl1/en/tv.action?pid=3310109601
- **US, BLS QCEW 2024 annual averages, private ownership:** 722320 caterers 13,816 establishments; 531120 lessors 27,222. https://data.bls.gov/cew/data/api/2024/a/industry/722320.csv (and /531120.csv)
- **US venue count, directory scrape:** about 35,829 US wedding venues, 71.8% (25,726) single-owner, late 2025, per RenTech Digital SmartScraper data cited by an investment blog [LQ]. https://www.mmcginvest.com/post/the-u-s-wedding-venue-market-a-investment-thesis-for-2026-2030 (updated 2025-08-14)
- **Canadian venue count:** [NE]. A WeddingWire.ca category page lists venues by city but does not publish totals.
- **Demand side:** about 2 million US weddings in 2025 (The Knot Worldwide, 2026 Real Weddings Study, 2026-02-18). https://www.theknotww.com/press-releases/the-knot-worldwide-unveils-2026-real-weddings-study

### B. Value per customer

- **US average venue cost: USD 12,900** for 2025 weddings (The Knot 2026 Real Weddings Study, 10,474 couples; page blocks direct fetch, figure read from its search listing). https://www.theknot.com/content/average-cost-reception-venue
- Average total wedding USD 34,000; 117 guests; USD 292 per guest (The Knot WW, 2026-02-18, link above).
- Rural raw-space example: USD 4,800 off-peak, USD 6,100 peak for ceremony plus reception (one sampled Ohio venue's Knot listing, 2026).
- **Canada:** CAD 5,000 to 20,000 venue ranges appear only in blog guides [LQ]. https://todaysbride.ca/articles/the-ultimate-wedding-budget-breakdown-canadian-edition/ . A primary Canadian venue-cost survey: [NE].
- **Payback [A]:** at a USD 3,000 to 5,000 site, one extra booking pays back at nearly any venue (a raw-space venue at USD 4,800 to 6,100 or an average venue at USD 12,900). Even assuming 30 to 50% contribution margin [A], one to two extra bookings a year covers it.

### C. Website role

- 90% of wedding planning happened online; couples spent about 6 hours a week researching vendors; 64% used planning sites or apps; 82% hired the venue first; engagement averaged 15 months (The Knot Real Weddings 2025 venue report, couples married in 2024, published 2025 by WeddingPro, which sells advertising [LQ for the framing, but it is the largest survey available]). https://pros.weddingpro.com/report/the-knot-real-weddings-2025-venue-report/
- 36% of engaged couples used AI in planning, nearly double the prior year; 52% said responsiveness built trust; 37% contacted more vendors than planned (The Knot WW, 2026-02-18, link above). This supports AI-search visibility and fast enquiry follow-up as selling points.
- **What the site must do:** starting price or pricing guide, capacity by space, date availability, tour booking, galleries and video, reviews, FAQs (catering rules, rain plan, lodging), and an enquiry form that captures date, guest count and budget. Sample finding (E): none of 10 venue homepages showed a starting price and only 2 of 10 showed capacity.

### D. Platform lock-in

- **Directories capture demand but do not supply the owned site.** The Knot and WeddingWire (both The Knot Worldwide) and Zola run lead marketplaces. Reported Knot venue pricing: free basic listing; USD 500 to 1,200 a month in competitive metros, often on 12-month contracts [LQ, venue marketing vendor]. https://www.fullybookedvenue.com/the-ultimate-guide-to-the-knot-vendor-pricing/ . Another vendor puts The Knot/WeddingWire at USD 200 to 2,000+ a month [LQ]. https://weddingvenueleads.com/blog/how-much-do-wedding-venues-spend-on-advertising (2026-04-15)
- A cited 2025 survey claims 66.7% of wedding pros did not make back their Knot/WeddingWire spend [LQ, source survey not examined]. Via search summary of https://www.wedypro.ai/blog/wedding-directory-ads-roi-the-knot-weddingwire-2026
- **Venue software** (Tripleseat, Perfect Venue, HoneyBook) sells CRM, proposals and payments; I found no evidence they bundle a public marketing website. Perfect Venue lists plans from about USD 99 to 139 a month (third-party listings vary). https://www.perfectvenue.com/pricing ; Tripleseat does not publish prices. https://tripleseat.com/packages/
- **Read:** strong motive to own demand. Venues pay four figures a year to directories they do not control, and the site is where a directory lead gets converted.

### G. Reachability

- **Communities:** "Wedding Venue Owners & Managers Community" Facebook group claims 7,300+ screened venue owners (group owner's site [LQ]). https://weddingvenueowners.com/wedding-venue-owners-managers-community/ ; The Venue Association (Facebook). - **Emails:** venue sites in the sample usually publish an events or info address [A from sample]. ### H. Budget evidence

- Venues "should allocate 5 to 10% of revenue" to marketing; small venues USD 1,000 to 3,000 a month, mid-size USD 3,000 to 7,000 [LQ, venue ad agency, no sources cited, 2026-04-15]. https://weddingvenueleads.com/blog/how-much-do-wedding-venues-spend-on-advertising
- Venue revenue tiers: rural USD 100,000 to 300,000 a year, suburban USD 400,000 to 700,000 [LQ, investment blog]. https://www.mmcginvest.com/post/the-u-s-wedding-venue-market-a-investment-thesis-for-2026-2030
- Directory spend of USD 6,000 to 12,000 a year in metros [LQ] (D above). If even half true, a USD 3,000 to 5,000 site is under one year of directory spend [A].

### I. Fit

- **Copy and offer explanation: high.** Packages, inclusions, catering rules and pricing are confusing; couples compare several venues; every sampled site hid price.
- **Search and AI visibility: high.** Queries like "barn wedding venue near [city]" and AI-assisted planning (36%).
- **Photography and video: high dependence.** Venues usually already have wedding photographer galleries they can license [A].
- **Seasonality:** 69% of weddings May to November, October about 17% [LQ, blog]. Owners are busiest in peak season; selling window is winter [A].
- **Compliance friction: low.** No advertising regime beyond general consumer law; accessibility expectations (AODA in Ontario, ADA risk in US) apply to all sites [A].
- **Cross-sell: strong.** SEO, GBP, enquiry intake, tour-booking reminders and follow-up sequences (responsiveness cited by 52% of couples).
- **Proof transfer: strong.** Matthew's musician site already has an event enquiry form asking date, venue and guest count, which is the same buyer and the same form logic.

### J. Counterevidence and risks

- Wedding demand is mature and flat to declining [A]; roughly 20% of new venues are said to fail in year one [LQ]. https://www.mmcginvest.com/post/the-u-s-wedding-venue-market-a-investment-thesis-for-2026-2030
- Many venues are pleased with directory leads and may see a site as secondary; some already use Showit/Squarespace with photographer-grade imagery (sample: 3 of 10 strong).
- Some venues are restaurants, hotels or corporate groups (excluded, but they dominate lists), reducing the pool of true owner-operators.
- Distribution in Canada is thin: no venue count; WeddingWire.ca Hamilton list was dominated by hotels, golf clubs and restaurant groups.
- Photography dependence: a site without great images underperforms regardless of copy.

## 2. Wineries, cideries, craft distilleries (small producers with tasting rooms)

### A. Size

- **NAICS:** 312130 wineries (US and Canada; cider is generally classed with wineries [A]); 312140 distilleries.
- **Canada, StatCan Canadian Business Counts, December 2025** (released 2026-02-13, WDS API): wineries 521 locations with employees plus 362 without; Ontario 184 plus 172. Distilleries 227 plus 185; Ontario 48 plus 69. Size mix for wineries with employees (Canada): 168 with 1 to 4, 108 with 5 to 9, 98 with 10 to 19, 98 with 20 to 49. Tables as linked in section 1A.
- **Industry counts, Canada:** 860 wineries; BC 328, Ontario 292 (2025 BMO Wine Report via Small Farm Canada, 2025-12-09). https://www.smallfarmcanada.ca/news/canadian-wineries-weather-storms-and-eye-growth-in-2026/ . Wine Country Ontario lists 56 wineries in the Niagara Benchlands region alone. https://winecountryontario.ca/region/niagara-escarpment-twenty-valley/ . Ontario cider: "over 60" to "close to 100" producers (Ontario Craft Cider Association pages, sources conflict). https://ontariocraftcider.com/members/
- **US:** 11,107 wineries at start of 2026, down 3% from 11,450 in 2025 (Wine Business Monthly via Wine Economist, 2026-03-10). https://wineeconomist.com/2026/03/10/numbers-2026/ . BLS QCEW 2024: 5,801 employer winery establishments; NY 287, MI 147, OH 126. https://data.bls.gov/cew/data/api/2024/a/industry/312130.csv . Michigan: 279 producers (WineAmerica 2025 economic impact study). https://wineamerica.org/economic-impact-study-2025/michigan-wine-industry-2025
- **US craft distillers:** 2,282 active in August 2025, down 25.6% from 3,069 a year earlier (ACSA Craft Spirits Data Project, 2025-10). https://americancraftspirits.org/craft-spirits-sales-see-second-year-of-decline/ ; QCEW 2024: 1,873 employer distillery establishments. https://data.bls.gov/cew/data/api/2024/a/industry/312140.csv

### B. Value per customer

- **Tasting room and club:** tasting room and wine club channels are 72% of DTC revenue; DTC is about 70% of premium winery sales; tasting room visits down about 2.1% trailing 12 months; about half of wineries unprofitable; club conversion rates "halved" over a decade (SVB 2026 DTC Wine Report, 450 family wineries, via Press Democrat, 2026-06-11). https://www.pressdemocrat.com/2026/06/11/silicon-valley-bank-dtc-wine-report/ and https://www.svb.com/dtc-report/
- **Average DTC bottle price shipped: USD 56.78** in 2025, up 11%; DTC shipping volume down 15% and value down 6% (Sovos ShipCompliant and WineBusiness Analytics, 2026 report, 1,300+ wineries). https://sovos.com/press-releases/2026-direct-to-consumer-wine-shipping-report-reveals-record-declines-as-market-downturn-deepens/
- **Canada:** tasting rooms are 32% of direct sales; clubs plus tasting rooms 58%; 44% of wineries get over half of revenue from DTC (2025 BMO report via Small Farm Canada, link above).
- **Club member value:** a widely repeated "USD 2,803 lifetime value" could not be traced to its source [NE]. Wine Business Monthly reports the average club had nearly 1,700 members at start of 2024 (via search summary; WBM article paywalled). https://www.winebusiness.com/wbm/article/315325
- **Payback [A]:** assume a small Ontario winery club member spends CAD 400 to 800 a year [A] at roughly 50% gross margin [A]. A USD 3,000 to 5,000 site needs roughly 6 to 15 retained club members, or several hundred extra tasting-room visitors at typical small-winery spend [A]. Winery weddings and private events (common in Niagara) change the math toward the venue case.

### C. Website role

- DTC dominance (above) means the site is the store, the reservation desk and the club sign-up. Ontario wine country draws "over 3 million" annual visitors (Wine Country Ontario, undated page, older data) and 2.6 million (Wine Growers Ontario, 2025 page) [sources conflict]. https://winecountryontario.ca/ontario-vqa-wine-facts/ ; https://www.winegrowersontario.ca/
- An older winery-visitor stat ("almost 70% said the winery website was very important to their decision to visit") comes from a 2015 blog [LQ, old]. http://winetrailtraveler.com/blog/2015/04/13/wine-tourism-statistics-from-winery-and-tourist-points-of-view/
- **What the site must do:** hours, tasting menu with prices, reservation booking (Tock, Commerce7), online store, club join, events calendar, private event/wedding enquiry, directions, age affirmation where appropriate. Sample: tasting prices shown on 3 of 10 homepages.

### D. Platform lock-in

- **Commerce7 acquired WineDirect's SaaS division (announced 2025-01-16); WineDirect Classic shuts down 2026-12-31.** https://commerce7.com/press/commerce7-acquires-winedirects-saas-division/ ; shutdown date per Next Gen Wine Marketing. https://nextgenwinemarketing.com/moving-from-winedirect-to-commerce7-online-store/
- Commerce7 is commerce, POS, CRM, club and reservations, and integrates into an external site (WordPress, Squarespace) rather than hosting the whole site (Next Gen Wine Marketing, link above). **Implication [A]:** wineries still on WineDirect hosted sites face a forced rebuild or re-integration before 2026-12-31. This is a time-limited trigger, and specialists are already marketing to it.
- Commerce7 plans: Lite USD 59 a month, no transaction fees, for wineries under USD 100,000 DTC revenue (launched 2026-01-19); Basic from USD 149 a month plus 1.5% transaction fee. https://www.commerce7.com/resources/commerce7-launches-59-month-lite-plan-for-small-and-emerging-wineries/ ; https://www.commerce7.com/pricing/
- Commerce7 lists design partners (a ready referral channel for competitors). https://www.commerce7.com/partners-designers
- Others: Tock (reservations), Vinoshipper, OrderPort, VineSpring, Shopify (seen in sample). Commerce7 is headquartered in Vancouver. https://www.vinesos.com/best-winery-dtc-software
- **Read:** commerce is locked into platforms, the marketing site is not. The platform integration is a technical hurdle a solo builder must master.

### H. Budget evidence

- **Average revenue, Canadian SME wineries: CAD 773,000 (2024); 50.2% profitable** (ISED Canadian Industry Statistics, NAICS 31213). https://ised-isde.canada.ca/app/ixb/cis/summary-sommaire/31213

### I. Fit

- **Copy: medium to high.** Story, place and experiences sell; tasting menus and club benefits need clear explanation.
- **Search and AI: high for tourism queries** ("best wineries in Niagara-on-the-Lake", "winery with food near me") [A].
- **Photography/video: very high dependence.**
- **Seasonality:** strong summer and harvest peaks; winter is quieter [A].
- **Compliance friction: moderate.** Ontario: advertising must follow AGCO Registrar's guidelines; no appeal to minors; no ads on websites directed primarily at minors. https://www.agco.ca/en/alcohol/responsibilities-and-resources/appendix-2-registrars-guidelines-advertising-and-promotion . US: TTB advertising rules for wine and spirits; industry codes (DISCUS for spirits, Wine Institute for wine) call for age affirmation on member websites and 73.8% adult audience placement. https://distilledspirits.org/code-of-responsible-practices/ ; https://wineinstitute.org/our-work/responsibility/social/ad-code/ . DTC shipping compliance is handled by platforms, but adds integration complexity [A].
- **Cross-sell:** SEO and content (tourism), email and club automation. Commerce7 and similar platforms already own much of the CRM and email automation, which narrows the automation upsell [A].
- **Local identity:** real and credible for Niagara wineries; weaker for selling to US wineries.

### J. Counterevidence and risks

- Industry contraction: US wineries down 3% (2025 to 2026); US craft distillers down 25.6% in a year; DTC shipping volume down 15% in 2025; half of wineries unprofitable (sources above).
- Canada is small: about 883 winery locations and 412 distillery locations (StatCan), about 292 Ontario wineries (BMO). A Canada-only focus could run out of prospects; thousands exist only when US is included.
- Established specialists and platform design partners already sell to this market, including a live campaign around the WineDirect shutdown.
- Several Niagara wineries are corporate-owned (Arterra, Andrew Peller) or have marketing staff, so decision-making is less "owner says yes".
- No existing proof in wine; commerce platform integrations are technical.

## 3. Event performers and entertainers (live musicians, bands, DJs, specialty acts)

### A. Size

- **NAICS:** 711130 musical groups and artists; 711510 independent artists, writers and performers (Canada splits 711511 to 711513; independent DJs are indexed to 711510 [LQ index site]). https://naics.askkodiak.com/naics/2022/1b1b93353a3937e218202dc48c7b27da . Some DJ companies may classify as personal services [A].
- **Canada, StatCan Canadian Business Counts, December 2025:** 711130 musical groups and artists: 604 locations with employees (517 of them 1 to 4 employees) plus 9,236 without employees; Ontario 257 plus 3,607. 711512 independent actors, comedians and performers: 1,203 plus 10,220; Ontario 461 plus 3,955. Tables as linked in 1A.
- **US, BLS QCEW 2024:** 711130 employer establishments 6,060 (Indiana 67, Ohio 94); 711510 employer establishments 40,861. https://data.bls.gov/cew/data/api/2024/a/industry/711130.csv
- **US nonemployers:** 907,569 nonemployer establishments in 711510 in 2023 with USD 25.4 billion receipts (Census Bureau, 2025-07). This includes writers and visual artists, so the performer share is unknown [NE]. https://www.census.gov/library/stories/2025/07/nes-gig-economy.html . US nonemployer count for 711130: not retrieved [NE].
- BLS: about 16,900 jobs for disc jockeys (except radio) in 2025 (via search summary of BLS OOH). https://www.bls.gov/ooh/media-and-communication/announcers.htm

### B. Value per customer

- **US average wedding DJ USD 1,800; live band USD 4,500** (The Knot 2026 Real Weddings Study, 2025 weddings; figures read from its search listing). https://www.theknot.com/content/average-cost-wedding-band-dj
- Sample prices: DJ packages from USD 1,300 (4-hour reception); a DJ day rate from USD 1,000 (sampled businesses, 2026).
- Corporate event fees: [NE].
- **Payback [A]:** a USD 3,000 to 5,000 site needs 2 to 3 extra DJ bookings, or 1 to 2 band bookings, at gross fee. After musician splits and costs, a band may need more [A].

### C. Website role

- DJs are chosen on personality (53% say personality was the deciding factor) and responsiveness (52%) (The Knot WW 2026-02-18, link in 1A). Video and reviews carry more weight than copy [A].
- Couples discover performers largely through directories and venue referrals (64% used planning sites or apps, 2025 report) [A inference].
- **What the site must do:** performance video above the fold, song lists, packages and a starting price, availability check, enquiry form capturing date, venue, guest count and event type, reviews, and venue/planner-facing pages. Sample: 1 of 10 showed a starting price.

### D. Platform lock-in

### I. Fit

- **Copy: medium.** Personality and video decide; copy matters for packages, FAQs and planner-facing pages.
- **Search and AI: medium.** "Wedding DJ [city]" and "wedding band [city]" searches exist, but directories rank strongly [A].
- **Photography/video: very high dependence.**
- **Seasonality:** wedding season plus holiday corporate season [A].
- **Compliance: very low.**
- **Cross-sell:** enquiry auto-reply, availability check, quote follow-up automation fit well; SEO budgets are small.
- **Proof transfer: strongest of all industries** (Matthew's musician service site with event enquiry form).

### J. Counterevidence and risks

- Low budgets and part-time owners; lapsed domains in the sample suggest churn and low willingness to maintain paid tools.
- Cheap substitutes (Bandzoogle, directory profiles, Instagram, DJ software widgets).
- Marketplaces are inexpensive, so "own your demand" is a weaker pitch than for venues.
- Buyers decide on video and personality, which Matthew cannot supply through copy alone.
- Pricing pressure: a USD 3,000 to 5,000 site is 1 to 3 gigs of revenue for a solo act, a large share of a USD 28,000 average [A].

## Cross-industry notes

- **One buyer journey links all three.** Couples book the venue first (82%), then performers; Niagara wineries often run weddings too (one sampled Niagara winery markets a wedding hall). A "wedding and event businesses" niche could share one enquiry-form system, one proof story and one content engine, with venues as the budget anchor [A].
- **Venues have the best economics; performers have the best proof; wineries have the best local story.** The performer site could be the entry proof for venue outreach, since the enquiry form logic (date, venue, guest count) is the same.
- **A time-limited winery trigger exists:** WineDirect Classic shuts down 2026-12-31, forcing migrations to Commerce7 and external sites. Specialists are already selling into it, and the window is closing, so it favours incumbents over a newcomer [A].
- **Universal outreach opener:** pricing clarity. No venue homepage showed a starting price; 1 of 10 performers and 3 of 10 wineries showed prices. Pricing FAQs and "what's included" copy are Matthew's strength and are visible from outside.
- **Sample caveats:** 30 homepages, one rater, homepage only. Directory ordering (WeddingWire paid placement) likely biases venue and performer samples toward stronger marketers; real-world quality is probably worse.
- **Data gaps:** no Canadian venue count or venue-cost survey; no US nonemployer count for 711130; no traceable wine club lifetime value; directory pricing for venues comes only from vendor blogs.
