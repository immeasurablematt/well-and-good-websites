# Vertical research: design-build contractors, auto detailing, independent auto repair

Researched 2026-09-26. Every URL below was accessed on 2026-09-26; publication or data dates are given where the source states them.

Labels: **[A]** assumption, **[NE]** no evidence found, **[LQ]** low-quality source (vendor, agency or marketplace marketing, or an unverified third-party summary). Money is USD unless marked CAD.

Website sample method (applies to every section E): the sample cities are Hamilton ON and Columbus OH. For each industry I took the first independent, locally based businesses in organic web search results for a plain query, skipping directories, chains and franchises, and scored each homepage from raw HTML, a text read and a 375 px mobile screenshot. Names and URLs are in a private file kept outside this repository. **Bias warning:** businesses that rank organically are more likely than average to have invested in their site, so these samples probably overstate quality. Businesses with no site, or with poor SEO, are mostly invisible to this method.

### B. Value per customer

- US median renovation spend was $20,000 in 2025, and the top 10% of projects reached $150,000 or more. [Hardware Retailing on the 2026 US Houzz & Home Study, published 2026-04-23](https://hardwareretailing.com/home-renovation-activity-stays-strong-in-2025-as-high-end-projects-gain-momentum/) (Houzz is also a platform vendor; the survey is large but self-published.)
- A Hamilton renovator publishes CAD ranges of $35,000 to $80,000+ for kitchens, $15,000 to $35,000+ for bathrooms and $50,000 to $120,000+ for basements (one firm's own claim). [Newfore](https://newfore.com/)
- Custom and new homes: the average construction cost is $428,215, the average sales price $665,298, the builder margin 11.0% and marketing 0.8% of the sales price (2024 survey). [NAHB Eye on Housing, 2025-01-23](https://eyeonhousing.org/2025/01/cost-of-constructing-a-home-in-2024/)
- Inground pools average about $65,909, in a range of $44,499 to $87,349. [Angi cost guide](https://www.angi.com/articles/how-much-does-it-cost-install-pool.htm) [LQ]
- The typical NAHB remodeler completed 15 jobs over $10,000 in 2024. [NAHB Eye on Housing, 2025-09-09](https://eyeonhousing.org/2025/09/who-are-nahb-remodelers/)

**Payback:** a $20,000 renovation at a 25% to 35% gross margin [A] leaves $5,000 to $7,000 of gross profit. **One extra job pays for a $3,000 to $5,000 site.** One custom home at an 11% margin leaves about $73,000. Pools: one job. Landscape design-build: likely one or two jobs [A]; typical project size is [NE] from primary data.

### C. Website role

- 91% of US homeowners who renovate hire professionals. [Hardware Retailing / Houzz 2026](https://hardwareretailing.com/home-renovation-activity-stays-strong-in-2025-as-high-end-projects-gain-momentum/)
- In a 2025 roofing homeowner survey, 79% ask for word-of-mouth referrals, 62% search the internet and 78% are "more likely to call" a contractor that shows pricing on its website. About 67% rate online reviews "very" or "extremely" important. [Roofing Contractor (BNP Media research with Owens Corning), 2025-04-01](https://www.roofingcontractor.com/articles/100649-2025-homeowner-roofing-survey-tracking-the-journey) (trade publication; roofing is a nearby category, not the same one.)
- Across local businesses, 97% of US consumers read reviews, 54% visit the business website after reading positive reviews, and 45% now use ChatGPT or other AI tools for local recommendations (up from 6%). [BrightLocal Local Consumer Review Survey, 2026-02-11, n=1,002 US adults](https://www.brightlocal.com/research/local-consumer-review-survey/) [LQ: vendor survey, but widely used]
- What the site must do: show a portfolio (project photos), explain the process, give budget ranges, prove licensing and warranty (in Ontario, HCRA licence and Tarion warranty appear on sample sites) and book a consultation. Referrals start the search, and the site then confirms or loses the lead [A].

### D. Platform lock-in

- **Houzz Pro:** Design plan $139/mo, Pro $279/mo, Teams from $559/mo. Only Teams includes a "Custom Website"; ads start at $685/mo extra. [Houzz Pro pricing](https://www.houzz.com/houzz-pro/pricing)
- **HomeStars** (Canada, owned by Angi since HomeAdvisor bought it in 2017) and **Angi/HomeAdvisor** (US) sell paid placement and leads. They capture demand, but they do not replace the contractor's own site. [Wikipedia: HomeStars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HomeStars)
- Houzz directory pages ranked first for both "design-build Hamilton" and "design-build Columbus" in my searches (observed 2026-09-26), and a likely lead-generation site ranked among the Hamilton results.
- **Verdict:** no bundled site is good enough to replace a custom one here. The competition is local agencies, not platforms. Half of the sample (5 of 10) credit a local web agency in the footer.

### G. Reachability

NARI has 32 chapters in 49 states; its member count is [NE] ([NARI](https://nari.org/About-NARI), [Business View, Feb 2025](https://businessviewmagazine.com/brochures/feb-2025/nari/)). - Emails: all 10 sample sites publish an email address, and 2 use the owner's first name.
- Compliance: CASL applies to Canadian commercial email (implied consent is narrow for cold outreach, so a published business address plus a message relevant to the business role is the usual basis). CAN-SPAM applies in the US (opt-out and sender identification).

### H. Budget evidence

- Canada 23611 (businesses with CAD 30K to 5M revenue, 2024): average revenue CAD 522.4K and advertising CAD 4.6K. The upper-middle quartile averages CAD 327K revenue and CAD 3.8K advertising; the top quartile CAD 1.59M and CAD 12.0K. The advertising figures carry StatCan's "E, use with caution" flag. [ISED Financial Performance Data, 23611](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/app/ixb/cis/performance/rev/23611) (government, good quality)
- Canada 56173 landscaping (2024): average revenue CAD 488.9K and advertising CAD 6.3K; top quartile CAD 1.43M and CAD 18.2K. [ISED 56173](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/app/ixb/cis/performance/rev/56173)
- US: median NAHB remodeler revenue is $1.7M, and 44% earn $1.0M to $4.9M. [NAHB 2025-09](https://eyeonhousing.org/2025/09/who-are-nahb-remodelers/) (association member census, medium quality; members skew larger than the average firm.)
- Takeaway: a CAD 5,000 site is more than a full year's typical advertising spend for a median Canadian firm, but only about one job's gross profit.

### I. Fit

- Copy and service explanation: high. The purchase is considered, costly and anxious (process, budget, timeline, permits).
- Search and AI visibility: high. Cost-guide and "how it works" content suits Matthew's content and SEO background.
- Photography: high dependence. Firms usually have phone photos of finished projects; professional photos are a cost the client must fund [A].
- Seasonality: pools and landscape design-build are strongly seasonal in Canada (they sell in winter and spring and build in summer) [A]. Interior renovation is steadier.
- Compliance: low. Licensing and warranty claims must be accurate (HCRA/Tarion in Ontario, state licences in the US).
- Cross-sell: strong. Next steps are SEO and content, then enquiry intake and qualification (budget and timeline screening), consultation booking and follow-up automation.

### J. Counterevidence and risks

- Search-ranked firms already have strong sites (9 of 10), many built by local agencies. The opening "your site is weak" is unavailable for them.
- Referrals lead (79% in the roofing survey), so owners may credit referrals rather than the site.
- The segment is well served by funded specialists (Hook Agency, Houzz Pro, lead-generation marketplaces), and bigger firms expect paid ads and SEO retainers.
- Long sales cycle and project-based cash flow; 2025 renovation spend was flat and 2026 plans were lower (planned median $15,000). [Hardware Retailing / Houzz](https://hardwareretailing.com/home-renovation-activity-stays-strong-in-2025-as-high-end-projects-gain-momentum/)
- The four sub-segments differ: pools and landscapes are seasonal and photo-led, while custom builders have long cycles and fewer, bigger decisions.

## 2. Auto detailing and paint protection shops (ceramic coating, PPF, window tint)

### A. Size

There is no clean NAICS code. In both Canada and the US, detailing sits in **811192 Car washes**, which also contains tunnel washes. Window tinting sits in **811122** (glass replacement, repair and tinting) ([NAICS index via askkodiak](https://naics.askkodiak.com/naics/2022/767d3eb7d137160e34f2e787041d1990)). PPF-only shops may be coded 811121 or 811199 [A].

| NAICS | Canada, employer locations (Dec 2025) | US, employer establishments |
|---|---|---|

| 811122 Auto glass, including tint | 1,125; Ontario 327 | 7,163 (QCEW 2024) |
| 811199 / 811198 All other auto repair | 1,270; Ontario 569 | 5,368 (CBP 2023) |

- US nonemployers in 81119 (other auto repair, which includes detailing): 97,480 establishments with $4.75B of receipts, or about $48,700 each (2023). [Census NS2300NONEMP](https://data.census.gov/table/NONEMP2023.NS2300NONEMP) Many are likely mobile solo detailers [A].
- Franklin County OH: 81 car-wash establishments (2024). [BLS QCEW](https://data.bls.gov/cew/data/api/2024/a/industry/811192.csv)
- The true count of detailing and PPF shops is [NE]. IBISWorld's 57,900 "car wash and auto detailing businesses" in the US figure is [LQ] (paywalled, reached only through a summary).
- Sources: [StatCan 33-10-1095-01](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/t1/tbl1/en/tv.action?pid=3310109501); [Census CBP 2023](https://data.census.gov/table/CBP2023.CB2300CBP).

### B. Value per customer

- Observed published prices: in Hamilton, detailing packages start at CAD 75 to 250 and specialty work tops out at a CAD 999 starting price; a mobile detailer starts detailing at CAD 130 and ceramic coating at CAD 300. In Columbus, a full detail is $325+ and ceramic coating $795+, and one shop finances jobs of $500 to $15,000. These are the businesses' own sites; URLs are in the private file.
- Vendor guides put ceramic coating at $500 to $3,000+, full-front PPF at $2,000 to $3,500 and full-car PPF at $6,000+. [Champion Window Tinting](https://www.championwindowtinting.com/paint-protection-film-cost-vs-ceramic-coating-2025) [LQ]
- **Payback:** a $800 ceramic job at a 60% gross margin [A] leaves about $480, so a $4,000 site needs **about 8 coating jobs**. A $2,500 full-front PPF job at a 50% margin [A] leaves about $1,250, so **3 to 4 PPF jobs**. Maintenance-wash customers add repeat value, but repeat rates are [NE] (one "IDA 2025 survey" retention figure found online could not be traced to IDA and is excluded).

### C. Website role

- Detailing-specific survey evidence on online research is [NE]. General local evidence: 97% read reviews and 54% visit the website after positive reviews. [BrightLocal 2026](https://www.brightlocal.com/research/local-consumer-review-survey/) [LQ]
- Brand installer pages tell buyers to check Google reviews, portfolios and certification. [Ceramic Pro](https://ceramicpro.com/how-to-find-the-best-ceramic-coating-installer-near-me/) [LQ]
- What the site must do: show before and after galleries, explain package tiers (ceramic vs PPF vs tint), give starting prices, show installer certifications (XPEL, SunTek, Opti-Coat, Ceramic Pro and System X all appear in the sample) and offer instant quotes or booking. Instagram often acts as the portfolio [A].

### D. Platform lock-in

- **Urable** (detailing and PPF CRM): Express $70/mo, Pro $110/mo (online booking, lead forms), Enterprise $183/mo. It includes **no website builder**. [Urable pricing](https://urable.com/pricing/)
- **Design Detail:** a website builder made for detailers. [designdetail.io](https://www.designdetail.io/) (price [NE])
- Film and coating brands run installer locators, which capture some demand [A].
- **Verdict:** nothing bundles a good site. The substitutes are Instagram plus DIY builders: 4 of the 9 working sample sites use Wix or GoDaddy.

### H. Budget evidence

- Canada 81119 (other auto repair, including car washes and detailing; businesses with CAD 30K to 5M revenue, 2024): average revenue CAD 383.1K and advertising CAD 6.9K. - Takeaway: a $3,000 to $5,000 site is 3% to 5% of revenue for a middle-sized detailer and several years of its advertising budget. Only PPF and tint shops in the upper quartiles fit the price point [A].

### I. Fit

- Copy: medium. Explaining ceramic vs PPF vs tint and how long each lasts helps, but buyers are visual and price-led.
- Search and AI: medium. "Ceramic coating near me" and "PPF [city]" are searchable, but the map pack and Instagram dominate [A].
- Photography: high dependence, but owners usually hold large photo libraries already. Easy to supply.
- Seasonality: strong in Canada (spring and summer peaks, winter slump) [A].
- Compliance: minimal. Warranty claims for coatings and film should match manufacturer terms.
- Cross-sell: booking and quote automation is plausible, but Urable-type tools already cover it for $70 to $183/mo.

### J. Counterevidence and risks

- Small budgets: the median detailer's revenue is near CAD 100K, and the market is dominated by solo operators.
- A cheap vertical supply ($599 sites, $49/mo builders) anchors prices low.
- Sample quality was already mostly strong or OK among ranked shops.
- Churn: small, often young businesses. Survival data is [NE].
- Low barriers to entry mean price shopping.

- Nonemployers: in Canada, 8111 as a whole has 23,576 "non-employer or indeterminate" establishments against 25,037 employers ([ISED 8111](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/app/ixb/cis/businesses-entreprises/8111)). In the US, 81111 has 207,501 nonemployers averaging about $60,600 in receipts (2023; [Census NES](https://data.census.gov/table/NONEMP2023.NS2300NONEMP)).
- Local markets: Hamilton CMA has 445 employer locations in 8111 ([StatCan 33-10-1097-01](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/t1/tbl1/en/tv.action?pid=3310109701)). Franklin County OH has 212 in 811111 (2024; [BLS QCEW](https://data.bls.gov/cew/data/api/2024/a/industry/811111.csv)).

### B. Value per customer

- In a 2025 survey of 752 US shops, 36% reported an average repair order of $500 to $749 and 34% reported $250 to $499. [PartsTech 2025 State of General Auto Repair, via search summary](https://partstech.com/resource/blog/how-to-increase-your-auto-repair-shops-average-repair-order-value-aro/) [LQ: vendor]
- **Payback:** a $450 repair order at a 55% gross profit [A] leaves about $250, so a $4,000 site needs **about 16 repair orders**. If a new customer returns twice a year [A], that is about 8 retained new customers in year one. Retention makes this plausible, but it is not "one job pays".

### C. Website role

- Auto-repair-specific primary survey evidence is [NE]. The figures that circulate ("90% read reviews before choosing a repair shop") come from vendor blogs [LQ].
- Kukui studied 700 shops in 30 states and found that 45% did not respond to any of their last 10 Google reviews and only 13% responded to all 10. [Kukui, 2025-02-19](https://www.kukui.com/google-review-response-rates-auto-repair-study) [LQ: vendor]
- What the site must do: list services, show reviews and certifications (ASE, AAA/CAA, NAPA), take online booking, publish hours and location, and explain warranty. Customers decide mainly on proximity, reviews and trust [A].

### D. Platform lock-in

- **Kukui:** bundles CRM, marketing and a website, and is a NAPA AutoCare certified partner ([Kukui NAPA](https://www.kukui.com/napa)). Pricing is not published (a competitor claims $500 to $2,000+/mo [LQ]).
- **Tekmetric:** the Marketing add-on costs "$345 per month per shop" and covers online booking, reminders and reviews. [Tekmetric pricing](https://www.tekmetric.com/pricing)
- **Drive (driveshops.app):** a shop website app ([Drive](https://driveshops.com/websites/)).

## Cross-industry notes

1. **Only one of the three clears the payback test with room to spare: design-build.** One job pays for the site. Detailing needs 3 to 8 jobs and auto repair about 16 repair orders [A margins].
2. **Search-ranked businesses already look good in all three** (21 of 30 sites strong). Outreach that opens with a critique works best on the unranked long tail, which this method could not sample. A second sample drawn from directory listings (Houzz page 3 or later, HomeStars, RenoMark, Google Maps results past the top 3) would test that directly.
3. **Budget reality (Canadian government data, 2024):** median-sized firms in all three industries spend only about CAD 1K to 7K a year on advertising. A CAD 4K to 7K site is a large decision everywhere, and it is easiest to justify where one job carries CAD 5K+ of gross profit (renovation, pools, custom homes, top-quartile PPF shops).
4. **Platform capture ranks** auto repair (high), then detailing (low bundling but a cheap vertical supply), then design-build (marketplaces sell leads, not sites; local agencies are the real competition).
5. **AI visibility is a live, timely angle:** 45% of US consumers now use ChatGPT or other AI tools for local recommendations (BrightLocal 2026 [LQ]). It fits considered purchases best, which again means design-build.
6. **Sample caveat:** the design-build sample covered renovators only. Landscape design-build and pool builders, which are seasonal and photo-led, were not sampled and may show more visible pain [A].
