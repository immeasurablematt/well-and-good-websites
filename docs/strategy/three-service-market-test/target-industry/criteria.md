# Target industry: first-principles criteria and screen

Situation: solo founder, about 10 hours a week, no income need, zero paying clients, bootstrapping. Lead service: websites (copy, design, build, local search foundations), with SEO/content and AI automation as later add-ons. Sell remotely to Canada and the US. Wants small and medium businesses, one industry.

## What has to be true for an industry to be the right first target

1. **A website visibly makes the owner money.** Their customers research and compare online before buying, and one extra customer is worth more than the site costs (high value per customer or per job).
2. **The owner can say yes.** Owner-led, 1 to 50 staff, one decision-maker, revenue big enough to spend USD 2,500 to 5,000 without a committee.
3. **The pain is visible from outside.** Many businesses in the industry have weak, outdated, or DIY sites that can be judged remotely, so outreach can open with something specific (a preview or a short critique).
4. **No platform already hands them a good-enough site.** Industries captured by vertical software or marketplaces (restaurants, realtors, funeral homes) get sites bundled cheaply.
5. **They can be found and reached remotely.** Public lists (associations, directories, Google Maps categories), communities, and published business emails. Enough of them in Canada and the US (thousands, not hundreds) that 10 hours a week never runs out of prospects.
6. **Low compliance friction.** No heavy advertising rules (law, finance, medical claims) or privacy regimes that slow a solo builder.
7. **Matthew's strengths matter.** Explaining services clearly, copy, search and AI visibility. Industries where the purchase is considered (people read before buying) reward this more than impulse or emergency purchases.
8. **Room to grow into the other two services.** After the site: search/content, then intake or follow-up automation.
9. **Proof can transfer.** Existing work (a performer's service site with event enquiry form, an auto repair concept, a sandwich shop concept) helps if the industry resembles it.
10. **Competition proves demand without closing the door.** Some vertical website agencies existing is good (buyers pay). A market saturated by well-funded vertical specialists is bad for a newcomer.

## Knock-out screen (judgment, to be challenged by research)

| Industry | Outcome | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Restaurants, cafes | Out | Thin margins; Toast, Owner.com, BentoBox and delivery apps bundle sites |
| Real estate agents | Out | Brokerage and IDX platforms; high agent churn |
| Lawyers, financial advisors | Out | Advertising rules; saturated vertical agencies |
| Dentists, optometrists, veterinarians, hearing clinics | Out | Saturated vertical platforms (ProSites and similar) and consolidation into chains |
| Funeral homes | Out | Vertical platforms dominate |
| Churches, parishes | Out | Low budgets, committee decisions, vertical platforms (eCatholic) |
| Fitness studios, martial arts, yoga | Out | Low budgets; gym software bundles sites |
| Daycares, private schools | Out | Regulation, waitlist-driven |
| Moving, cleaning, pest control, solar, emergency plumbing | Out | Price-shopped or lead-marketplace driven; saturated specialist agencies |
| Pet grooming, dog training | Out | Low budgets |
| Tour operators, B&Bs | Out | OTA and booking-software dependence |
| Med spas | Out for now | Health-claim rules; saturated |
| Therapists, physio, chiro | Out for now | Directory- and booking-software dependent; low budgets for solo practices |
| **Design-build contractors** (renovation, custom homes, landscape design-build, pools) | Shortlist | High-ticket, considered, visual; website and reviews central |
| **Auto detailing and paint protection shops** (ceramic, PPF, tint) | Shortlist | Owner-led, mid to high ticket, visual, many DIY sites |
| **Independent auto repair** | Shortlist | Existing concept; recurring customers; check vertical agencies |
| **Wedding and event venues** | Shortlist | Very high value per booking; website central to shortlisting |
| **Wineries, cideries, distilleries** | Shortlist | Niagara story is authentic; tourism, events, clubs; check platforms |
| **Event performers and entertainers** | Shortlist | Closest to existing proof; check budgets and directory dependence |
| **Small manufacturers and job shops** (machining, fabrication) | Shortlist | High value per customer; many outdated sites; RFQ-driven; content matters |
| **Accounting and bookkeeping firms** | Shortlist | High lifetime value per client, owner-led; check referral dependence and template vendors |
