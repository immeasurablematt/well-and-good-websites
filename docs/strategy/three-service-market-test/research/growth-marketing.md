# Growth marketing line: market research (Well and Good Growth)

Researched 2026-09-25. All URLs accessed 2026-09-25 unless noted. Currency is stated per figure. "Assumption" and "arithmetic" labels mark anything that is not a sourced fact. Vendor and agency statistics are flagged as low quality.

Sub-offers are treated separately throughout:
- **SEO / local SEO** (website SEO, Google Business Profile (GBP), citations, reviews)
- **Content** (pages, articles, sales collateral)
- **Social** (organic posting and management)
- **AI-search / GEO visibility** (being cited or recommended by ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Google AI Overviews / AI Mode)
- **Fractional marketing leadership** (strategy, planning, part-time CMO or growth lead)

## 1. Buyer and triggers

**Buyer problem by sub-offer**

| Sub-offer | Problem the buyer names | Typical trigger | Decision-maker | Realistic first engagement |

Storyteller Media sells one-time GBP optimization at CAD 345 and managed GBP at CAD 285/mo |
| Website SEO | Traffic or leads flat or falling | Redesign or migration, traffic drop after a Google update, new service line | Owner or marketing manager | Audit or project first (Storyteller audits CAD 1,495; Ahrefs survey most common project fee USD 2,501 to 5,000), retainer after |
| Content | "We need to be posting / publishing but have no time" | Sales team needs collateral, launch, fundraising, SEO recommendations requiring pages | Marketing lead or founder (B2B); owner (SMB) | Defined batch of pages or a sales deck (project), then per-piece or monthly |
| Social | "Our pages are dead"; owner guilt about not posting | Owner can no longer keep up; new hire left; event season (tourism in Niagara) | Owner | Monthly retainer is the norm; Cool Koala requires a 2-month minimum |
| AI-search / GEO | "Does ChatGPT know we exist?" | Owner or exec tries asking ChatGPT or Claude and doesn't appear; press coverage of AI search | Owner (SMB); CMO / head of growth (B2B, enterprise) | Visibility check or audit bundled into SEO; standalone add-on averages USD 937/mo in one small agency survey (see section 2d) |
| Fractional leadership | "Nobody owns marketing"; spend with no plan | Funding round, first marketing hire needed, agency churn, founder burnout | Founder / CEO | 3 to 6 month retainer; Fractional Jobs reports most engagements last 6 to 12 months |

**Main substitutes (all sub-offers)**
1. **DIY by the owner.** CFIB (Sept 2025, n=2,478 Canadian independent business owners): 9 in 10 use at least one digital channel, 78% have a website, 52% use GBP/Maps, 59% Facebook; "lack of time" is the top barrier for engaged users (66%). Constant Contact (Q1 2026, n=1,500+ across US, Canada, UK, AU, NZ): 74% expect to spend more time on marketing themselves; 54% already use AI marketing tools, 44% use AI to write content.
2. **SaaS tools replacing done-for-you work.** Localo from USD 39 to 49/mo for one GBP with AI posts and review management; BrightLocal platform plans (price on request) with a "Local AI Visibility Tracker"; Semrush AI Visibility Toolkit launched at USD 99/mo per domain (2025). GoDaddy is de-emphasising "do-it-for-you services" in favour of its Airo AI agent (Q2 2026).
3. **Cheap freelancers and offshore firms.** Fiverr search freelancers report typical GEO project costs of USD 150 to 300; offshore firms run Niagara city landing pages (Digital Piloto shows an Indian company registration). CheckSite (St. Catharines) sells SEO from CAD 30 to 55/mo.
4. **Local agencies.** Cool Koala (Niagara social from CAD 950/mo), Asset Digital (Toronto), Digital Estate Media (Mississauga). See section 3.
5. **National / US productized.** Merchynt GBP management USD 449/mo, BrightLocal Managed SEO USD 1,299/mo.
6. **In-house hire.** Job Bank median for sales and marketing coordinator (NOC 11202), Hamilton-Niagara Peninsula: CAD 33.33/hr (range 22.45 to 57.86). Arithmetic: 33.33 x 2,000 hrs = about CAD 66,700/yr before benefits.
7. **Doing nothing.** A real option: 1 in 10 CFIB respondents are offline, 55% of those say digital is not relevant to their customers.

### (b) Search interest (supplied DataForSEO data, interpreted)

- **Local demand is tiny.** "seo niagara" 70/mo, "seo st catharines" 20, "digital marketing niagara" 10. Combined about 100 searches/mo across the region. Inbound search is not a volume channel locally.
- **GTA is the local search market.** "seo toronto" 4,400/mo, roughly 44x the Niagara terms combined (arithmetic).
- **US is about 9x to 11x Canada** on shared terms ("seo services" 60,500 vs 6,600; "local seo" 8,100 vs 880; "digital marketing agency" 40,500 vs 3,600).
- **"Growth marketing" is not buyer vocabulary.** "growth marketing agency" 70/mo in Canada vs "digital marketing agency" 3,600 and "seo services" 6,600.
- **GEO search volume is real but mid-sized.** Canada "ai seo" 880 and "generative engine optimization" 590 vs "seo services" 6,600 (about 13% and 9%, arithmetic). US ratios are similar (11% and 7%). Assumption: a large share of these searches are from marketers learning the term, not SMB buyers. No data separates the two.
- "google business profile optimization" 320/mo in Canada; "social media management" 720.

## 6. Delivery economics

**Time to visible outcomes**
- Google (Search Central "Do you need an SEO?", as quoted in Maile Ohye guidance): "in most cases the SEO will need four months to a year" to implement improvements and see benefit. Current page (updated 2026-06-05) was fetched but the wording **could not be re-confirmed** by the fetch tool; the quote is confirmed via secondary sources.
- Ahrefs poll (about 3,680 to 4,300 respondents on LinkedIn and X; self-selected SEOs): most say 3 to 6 months.
- GBP / map pack: vendor claims of 60 to 90 days (low quality).
- Social: Cool Koala claims results within the first month (vendor).
- AI-search: no credible time-to-result evidence found.

**Churn and retention (all low quality unless noted)**
- Focus Digital report (Jul 6 2026; **sample size and method not disclosed**): annual churn SEO 38%, content 35%, social 46%, PPC 49%, full-service 25%; agencies with 1 to 10 staff 32%, 51+ staff 15%; retainer agencies lose about 8% of clients in months 1 to 6.
- SE Ranking (n=260, Dec 2024, not statistically significant): 50% of agencies retain clients 2+ years.
- Thryv (public filing, Q2 2026): seasoned net revenue retention 90% for SMB SaaS. Best-quality churn proxy found for SMB marketing spend, but it is software, not services.
- Yext: total ARR down 1% from "contraction among smaller customers".

**Recurring margin and scope creep (arithmetic and assumptions)**
- Grow at CAD 699/mo includes local SEO, 2 content pages, about 12 posts on 2 platforms, review management, monthly report and a site. Market price for 12 social posts alone at Asset Digital is CAD 1,500 to 1,999; a 1,000-word page costs USD 60 to 160 from Verblio before strategy or editing. Assumption: if the founder's target is CAD 100/hr, CAD 699 buys about 7 hours/month for all of that scope. Profitability depends on heavy templating and AI assistance. **No evidence found** on actual hours per client.
- Reporting: every Grow and Dominate tier includes a monthly report; benchmark hours per report **not found**.
- Scope creep risk is highest in social (revisions, photography, comment management) and content (approvals). Asset Digital and Cool Koala both price photo and video separately or at higher tiers.

**Attribution limits**
- AI referrals are hard to measure: Similarweb says AI referrals remain "a low single-digit percentage of most sites' total traffic". Conductor (13,770 US domains, May to Sept 2025): 1.08% of sessions from AI referrals. BrightEdge (Jan to Aug 2025): under 1% of referrals.
- Ahrefs (300,000 keywords, Dec 2023 vs Dec 2025 GSC data): top-ranking pages see about 58% lower CTR when AI Overviews appear on informational queries. SEO work can improve rankings while clicks fall, which drives attribution disputes.

## 7. Counterevidence

1. **Slow results vs short patience.** Google's own guidance is four months to a year; low-quality churn data puts SEO around 38%/yr and social around 46%/yr, with early months highest risk.
2. **Price floor collapse.** CheckSite CAD 30 to 55/mo locally; Localo USD 39 to 49/mo; Fiverr GEO USD 150 to 300 per project; 61% of Fiverr search freelancers say clients expect lower prices because AI is involved. Upwork says lower-complexity work is shifting to automation.
3. **Platforms absorbing the work.** GoDaddy Airo (5x bookings in a quarter) and Thryv's AI platform target the same owner-led SMB. Thryv's done-for-you marketing services revenue fell 62% YoY.
4. **Flat local SMB budgets.** Yelp: paying ad locations down 1%, services advertising flat, management expects a challenging environment for local businesses through 2026. Yext: smaller customers contracting.
5. **Clicks are shrinking even when rankings are good.** Ahrefs: 58% lower CTR with AI Overviews (informational queries). Whitespark (Q2 2025): AI Overviews appear on 68% of local searches overall, but only 15% of simple local-intent queries, so the map pack still matters most for "service + city" searches.
6. **AI content commoditization.** Google ran spam updates in March, June and August 2026 targeting scaled content abuse; AI content is allowed if helpful, but mass-produced pages carry demotion risk. Content-as-volume is not a moat.
7. **Local proof gap.** The founder's growth-marketing proof is B2B tech startup content. The only local SMB proof is one website client. Local buyers will ask for local results.
8. **GEO hype risk.** AI referrals are around 1% of traffic; 61% of Fiverr search freelancers still see Google SEO as the stronger long-term investment.

**Evidence that AI-search demand is real (for balance):**
- BrightLocal (US, n=1,002, Feb 11 2026): 45% of consumers used AI tools to find local businesses (up from 6%); ChatGPT used by 31%; Google's share as top review platform fell from 83% to 71%.
- Leger for National Bank (Canada, n=1,518, June 5 to 7 2026): 39% of Canadians used generative AI in purchase decisions in the past 12 months; Ontario 41%.
- Similarweb (Jul 29 2026): AI referral visits 770.7M/mo worldwide (Jun 2025 to May 2026), up 117% YoY; ChatGPT citation rate for professional services under 4%.
- Shopify Q2 2026: AI-referred sessions up 197%, but organic search still referred more sessions than all AI platforms combined.
- Paying evidence: Digital Estate Media sells GEO at CAD 1,500/mo; SE Ranking agencies average USD 937/mo for AIO services; Fiverr GEO services up 29% in six months. **No evidence found** of how many Canadian SMBs actually pay for GEO.

## 8. Geography

- **Local identity:** Niagara competitors (CheckSite, Cool Koala, NSO) lead with Niagara town names; offshore firms copy that with city landing pages. No evidence found that "local" raises close rates or prices. Search demand for Niagara SEO terms is about 100/mo combined, so local identity helps with warm and referral sales more than with inbound.
- **Remote delivery is normal:** Storyteller Media (Nova Scotia) sells nationally; Digital Estate Media covers Ontario from Mississauga; Merchynt and BrightLocal sell GBP and local SEO remotely.
- **GTA:** "seo toronto" 4,400/mo; Toronto providers price SEO at CAD 1,500 to 6,000+/mo (Asset Digital, Digital Estate Media), well above current Well and Good pricing.
- **US:** search volume 9x to 11x Canada; US productized prices in USD (Merchynt USD 449, BrightLocal USD 1,299 for SEO only). US fractional marketing averages USD 209/hr. US local SMB budgets are flat (Yelp). The B2B startup segment is US-heavy (Contra, Fractional Jobs), which matches the founder's network and allows USD pricing.
- **Compliance:** CASL governs outbound email from Canada to Canadian recipients; conspicuous-publication implied consent needs documentation.

## 10. Weakest evidence and biggest unknowns

**Weakest evidence used**
1. **Churn figures** (Focus Digital SEO 38%, social 46%) have no disclosed method or sample. Treat as directional only. Thryv's 90% NRR is the only audited SMB retention figure, and it is software.
2. **Canadian price bands** (CAD 800 to 2,500/mo; audits CAD 300 to 2,000) come from agency blogs with no method.
3. **GEO demand** data is either consumer-side (BrightLocal US, Leger Canada), enterprise-side (Conductor), or freelancer-reported (Fiverr, no n). Nothing measures Canadian SMB purchases of GEO.
4. Several figures came through search summaries where the primary page returned 403 or timed out (Conductor, Shopify, Gartner, Yext, GoDaddy, Semrush Q2 2025, Job Bank). Those rows are marked in the source list.

**Could not verify (June 2026 figures)**
- Asset Digital **CAD 795 SEO**: not found on either pricing page (seen: "local SEO starts at CAD 750/month" in text, packages from CAD 2,500).
- Storyteller **CAD 285/mo** is verified but is managed GBP, not local SEO; the company is in Nova Scotia.
- Niagara Stands Out **CAD 397/mo** local SEO appeared only in a search snippet.

**Biggest unknowns**
1. What share of Niagara and GTA SMBs pay an outside provider for SEO, social or content, and how much. **No evidence found.**
2. Close rates, sales cycles and deal sizes for any segment. **No evidence found**; would need Well and Good's own pipeline data.
3. Actual delivery hours per Grow or Dominate client, which decides whether current pricing is profitable.
4. Whether the founder's B2B startup network converts to paid fractional or content work, and at what rate. The fractional-market evidence (94% win via network) is favourable but is about fractionals in general.
5. Whether AI-search visibility changes SMB lead flow in measurable ways. Referral share is around 1% of traffic and attribution is weak.
