## Connected decisions

- **Customer need to website message:** use R01 to test the relevance of S02's
  practical-help angle. Record whether an observation comes from a customer,
  Matthew's recollection, a business preference, or an editorial hypothesis.
- **Offer to conversion path:** use S01 and S03 together. A website preview and
  an automation conversation are different first steps. The form is an enquiry,
  not a booked appointment. Keep package values in the existing builder.
- **Company story to proof:** S08 supplies concrete earlier work for the founder
  and growth pages. Preserve its attribution and metric definitions. R02 records
  why selected proof is usable and which claims were not selected.
- **Automation to demonstration:** R01 identifies interview preparation and
  project-context updates as useful work Matthew already performs with AI.
  R02 records implementation evidence and the manual hub rebuild boundary.
  S03 describes that experience without private project material. The separate
  research/content/data picker examples remain labelled illustrative.

## Voice and expression

S01 owns the established style rules. S03 provides working examples such as
the concrete task descriptions in the automation hero and the direct working
relationship in the founder section. S02 explains why those choices were made.
These draft examples still need Matthew's editorial judgment; they are not a
new approved voice corpus. Avoid deriving numerical writing quotas from a few
examples or importing writing from another business.

## Completion criteria

1. Record the usable customer evidence and identify what remains an assumption.
2. Confirm which customer need and type of engagement the site should prioritize.
3. Connect the recommended message to the offer, company story and available proof.
4. Revise the supporting copy and automation example within the agreed scope.
5. Build, validate and review the affected pages before calling the revision ready.

Update this map when a source or decision changes. Preserve original evidence
and superseded decisions rather than silently converting drafts into approvals.
