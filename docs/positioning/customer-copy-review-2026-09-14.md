# Customer copy revision

September 14, 2026. Local review draft. Supersedes the expression of the
September 14 first draft while retaining its audience, offers and proof.

## Correction

The earlier homepage opened with what Matt builds and handles. Its service
descriptions and founder section repeated that perspective. The visitor had
to translate a list of activities into a reason to buy.

Lead with the work the visitor can hand over and the business purpose it
serves. Name AI automation, websites and marketing clearly. Use experience
and examples to support trust after establishing the value to the customer.
First-person evidence and process explanations remain appropriate where they
help the visitor understand responsibility; they do not lead the sales message.

Matthew's follow-up correction makes expertise central to the value. Website
and growth customers need the knowledge and judgment to achieve a strong
result, as well as help delivering the work. Explain how experience, current
technology and sound marketing practice serve their business. Convenience
alone is insufficient. Keep the time-saving purpose of Agentic Ops, and keep
the decade of experience explicitly attributed to marketing.

## Guidance reviewed

- [NN/g: Homepage Design, 5 Fundamental Principles](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/homepage-design-principles/).
  Explain the offering and its value quickly, use familiar language, provide
  representative examples, and give links descriptive labels. Applied to the
  opening, service routes and proof link. The existing visual design was not
  redesigned in this copy pass.
- [GOV.UK: Writing for user interfaces](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/design/writing-for-user-interfaces).
  Write for scanning, put important information first, and keep the purpose
  of headings and links clear. Applied to the shorter hero, explicit enquiry
  action and separate location note. This is service-design guidance, not
  evidence that a particular marketing message will convert.
- [CXL: Creating a value proposition](https://cxl.com/blog/value-proposition-examples-how-to-create/).
  Explain relevance and the concrete customer benefit in language the buyer
  understands. Applied using the existing owner interview and supported
  services. No quantified benefit or competitive superiority was invented.

The managed Copywriting skill owns the revision. Its benefit-to-proof method,
website playbook and conversion-editing checks were used alongside the
project's Copywriting and CRO guidance. The earlier owner interview remains
the audience evidence. These guides are writing methods, not customer research.
