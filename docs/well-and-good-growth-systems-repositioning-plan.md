# Well & Good Growth Systems Repositioning Plan

*Created: 2026-07-15*

## Decision

Reposition and rename the existing business under a broader umbrella rather than launching a separate venture.

**Working name:** Well & Good Growth Systems
**Descriptor:** Websites, search visibility, and AI-powered operations.

This gives the business room to sell:

1. Websites as an accessible entry offer
2. SEO and AEO as recurring growth services
3. Agentic operations automation as the highest-value offer
4. Catholic Web Stewardship as a specialized vertical

A separate automation company would split attention, proof, domain authority, and lead generation before there is evidence that buyers need separate branding. The automation practice can be spun out later if it develops a distinct enterprise audience.

## Why the Current Site Is Limiting the Business

The problem is larger than a few displayed prices. The site currently positions Well & Good as the inexpensive website option:

- The homepage promises “Custom sites in 24 hours” and “80% less than agency pricing” at `index.html:196`.
- It frames published low pricing as a primary differentiator at `index.html:267-290`.
- The main offer ladder tops out at $899 per month at `index.html:324-376`.
- The contact form forces prospects into those plans at `index.html:397-403`.
- Structured data tells search engines the company’s range is $99 to $1,497 at `index.html:51`.
- Supporting pages reinforce “affordable,” “one-page,” and “from $99/month.”
- The current sitemap contains only four website-oriented pages. There is no dedicated SEO, AEO, automation, or operations page.

A qualified automation buyer could reasonably conclude that the company builds cheap local websites, not that it can redesign a revenue or operations workflow.

## New Positioning

The homepage should no longer lead with a deliverable or price. It should lead with the business outcome.

### Working homepage message

> **Build a business that gets found, wins customers, and runs with less manual work.**

Supporting message:

> Well & Good builds websites, search visibility, and AI-powered operating systems for businesses ready to grow without adding unnecessary overhead.

The site should present three connected capabilities.

### 1. Digital Foundation

- Custom websites
- Landing pages
- Conversion paths
- Hosting and technical maintenance
- Analytics and tracking

Affordable websites remain available here. They are no longer the definition of the company.

### 2. Search and Authority

- Technical and on-page SEO
- Local SEO
- Google Business Profile
- AEO and AI search visibility
- Content systems
- Schema and entity signals
- Citation monitoring

This should be positioned as helping companies get found through both traditional and AI-mediated discovery.

### 3. Agentic Operations

- Lead intake and qualification
- Automated research and reporting
- CRM and inbox workflows
- Document generation
- Knowledge systems
- Meeting follow-up and task routing
- Custom agents with human approval controls
- Monitoring and recurring business processes

This is the high-value offer. The site should describe it in terms of hours eliminated, response time improved, opportunities recovered, and operational capacity created.

## Removing Pricing Without Damaging AEO

Visible pricing helps AI systems compare services, but the current prices anchor the company at the wrong level. The business objective takes priority over machine-readable price transparency.

Do not leave the old prices in a hidden `pricing.md`, `pricing.txt`, structured-data block, or other machine-readable file. That would continue teaching agents that the company is a $99-per-month provider.

Replace dollar figures with a transparent **How Engagements Work** page.

### Website projects

Clearly scoped builds for organizations that need a stronger foundation. Lightweight projects remain available when that is genuinely all the client needs.

### Search growth

Ongoing SEO, local visibility, content, and AEO programs based on market competition, geographic scope, and publishing requirements.

### Operations systems

Discovery, implementation, and ongoing improvement for workflows where automation can recover time, reduce mistakes, or increase capacity.

### Pricing-related questions to answer without publishing anchors

- What determines the cost?
- Do you offer project and retainer work?
- Can you start with one workflow?
- Are affordable websites still available?
- What is included in discovery?
- When does automation produce a worthwhile return?

This remains clear and extractable for AI systems while preserving the ability to scope a lightweight website, a search engagement, or a larger automation system appropriately.

## AEO Strategy for the Repositioned Site

The site should demonstrate the AEO work the company sells.

### Create authoritative service pages

Each service page should open with a concise, self-contained definition and then provide examples, limitations, implementation details, and evidence.

Priority questions include:

- What is AEO?
- How is AEO different from SEO?
- What is agentic operations automation?
- Which business processes can AI agents automate?
- How much human oversight should an AI workflow have?

### Build three topic clusters

#### AEO and AI visibility

- AEO vs SEO
- How businesses appear in ChatGPT answers
- Local SEO for AI search
- Schema for AI visibility
- How to measure AI citations
- What `llms.txt` does and does not do
- AI visibility audit methodology

#### Agentic operations

- AI automation vs traditional automation
- Processes that should not be automated
- Human approval in agentic workflows
- Automating lead intake
- Automating meeting follow-up
- Connecting AI agents to existing software
- Security and account-access practices
- Calculating automation ROI

#### Websites and conversion

- When a one-page site is enough
- Website redesign vs targeted improvements
- SEO migration requirements
- Local service website architecture
- Conversion paths for service businesses

### Add strong trust signals

Every insight article should include:

- Author: Matthew Baggetta
- Relevant experience
- Published and updated dates
- Sources for external claims
- Original screenshots or workflow diagrams
- Clearly explained methodology
- Links to related services and case studies

The site also needs updated Organization, Service, Article, FAQ, and Breadcrumb schema. The current hard-coded `Offer` and `priceRange` data must be removed as part of the pricing scrub.

## Implementation Sequence

### Phase 1: Remove the damaging anchors immediately

- Remove every public dollar figure.
- Remove `Offer`, `price`, `priceSpecification`, and `priceRange` schema.
- Replace “Plans” navigation with “Services.”
- Remove “80% less than agency pricing.”
- Remove the agency-price comparison table.
- Replace the plan selector in the contact form with a needs selector:
  - Website
  - SEO and search visibility
  - AEO and AI visibility
  - Operations automation
  - Not sure yet
- Change “free website preview” to a broader diagnostic or working session.
- Update titles, meta descriptions, Open Graph copy, Twitter copy, CTAs, FAQs, and thank-you messaging so no old pricing or narrow positioning remains.

This prevents further low-price anchoring while the larger repositioning is developed.

### Phase 2: Establish the umbrella positioning

- Validate **Well & Good Growth Systems** for corporate-name, domain, social, and trademark conflicts.
- Keep the existing visual identity initially.
- Change the wordmark and descriptor.
- Write the new homepage around the three capabilities.
- Create the four service pages.
- Update About and Contact.
- Create `.agents/product-marketing.md` as the canonical positioning context for future marketing work.

### Phase 3: Preserve existing search equity

Do not discard `wellandgoodwebsites.ca`.

- Keep the existing domain active.
- Preserve useful URLs.
- Migrate to the new primary domain only after the new name is selected and the replacement site is ready.
- Add one-to-one 301 redirects.
- Update canonical URLs, schema, sitemap, Search Console, profiles, and citations.
- Keep `/affordable-website-design/`, `/one-page-websites/`, and `/web-design-niagara/` as focused acquisition pages.
- Reframe those pages under the larger company instead of deleting them.

### Phase 4: Build proof for the high-value offer

Before making automation the entire homepage, produce two or three concrete demonstrations:

- Lead intake to qualification and follow-up
- Meeting transcript to summary, tasks, and CRM updates
- Recurring research and reporting workflow
- Inbox or document-processing workflow

Each automation case study should show:

1. The original manual process
2. The system architecture
3. Where human approval remains
4. Tools and integrations used
5. Time or error reduction
6. The business result

This proof will do more for high-value sales than generic claims about AI automation.

### Phase 5: Publish the authority layer

- Add `/llms.txt` with accurate company and service context.
- Expand the XML sitemap.
- Implement Service, Organization, Article, FAQ, and Breadcrumb schema.
- Add author identity and updated dates.
- Publish direct-answer FAQs.
- Build the AEO and operations topic clusters.
- Check target queries monthly across Google, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Claude.
- Record citation, mention, and recommendation rates separately.

## What Not to Do

- Do not launch a second full company and maintain two sites now.
- Do not make “AI” the business name. Tools and terminology will change.
- Do not lead with cheap websites, even if they remain available.
- Do not call automation a vague “AI transformation” service.
- Do not bury websites entirely. They are a useful entry product and visible proof of execution.
- Do not migrate domains before redirects, canonicals, and the new information architecture are ready.
- Do not retain old machine-readable pricing after removing visible pricing.
- Do not produce separate thin content “for AI.” Write useful content for people and organize it clearly enough for search and answer engines to extract.

## Strategic Model

> **Well & Good Growth Systems** is the company.
>
> **Websites** establish the foundation.
>
> **SEO and AEO** create demand and authority.
>
> **Agentic operations** help the client handle that growth efficiently.
>

This is one coherent business rather than three unrelated services. The overall promise is:

> **Get found, get chosen, and operate better.**

Complete a pricing-and-positioning scrub of the existing pages first. Then build the new homepage and service architecture. Finalize the legal and domain name only after availability checks, but use **Well & Good Growth Systems** as the working positioning direction.
