# Site positioning and copy review

Status: review draft. This revision is prepared for a preview, not approved for production publication.

## Copy direction

The approved audience remains businesses across Niagara and the GTA. The site introduces three related services: websites that explain the business and lead to enquiries, marketing that helps customers find and understand those services, and automation for repeated administrative tasks. Direct access to Matt connects the three offers.

The intended action is an enquiry. Website visitors can request a free private preview; automation visitors can request a free first conversation. Neither action books a calendar slot or starts paid work. Packages, pricing, and terms remain as displayed in the existing Growth builder.

The leading angle is relatability: the business owner has work they need a hand with. The supporting angle is local connection and a direct working relationship. These are editorial choices within the approved positioning, not findings from customer research or measured conversion improvements.

| Angle | Fit for this revision | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Relatability | High | Concrete tasks, customer questions, and repeated admin |
| Belonging | Medium | Matt in Welland, serving Niagara and the GTA |
| Curiosity | Medium | Free website preview and interactive automation examples |
| Challenge | Low | No confrontation or criticism of the visitor |
| Loss aversion | Low | No invented costs of delay or lost-sales claims |
| Fear with efficacy | Low | No manufactured threat; show an achievable next step |

## Page coverage

- Homepage: preserve the headline, sharpen the supporting promise, clarify the service paths, shorten the founder section, bring Frank’s reported outcome into the work section, and replace the generic closing invitation.
- Website service: make the free preview the main action; explain copy, enquiries, mobile checks, scope, and existing-system compatibility.
- Growth marketing: connect services and content to the customers the business wants to reach; explain the work and how available evidence guides it.
- Automation: start with one repeatable task, make examples clearly illustrative, explain scope and testing, and retain human review and cost boundaries.
- Niagara, Welland, and St. Catharines website pages: give each a distinct emphasis while retaining truthful location information and shared offers.
- Niagara SEO: replace keyword repetition and implied ranking promises with the actual local-search work and customer decisions.
- Frank Baggetta: clarify attribution and the before/after narrative while preserving the testimonial and launch-period evidence.
- Contact, service-aware forms, thank-you, and missing-page copy: make the next step and expected response clear. The privacy page was reviewed; its main content remains unchanged.

## Evidence and constraints

Sources: `.agents/product-marketing.md`, `docs/copy-deck-status.md`, and the current Growth builder at main commit `871a8bf`. The live homepage was inspected before revision. Historical July decks were not used as current offers or proof.

All displayed prices, plan features, annual amounts, setup charges, and cancellation terms are preserved. Frank’s testimonial, numerical statistics, and the source note covering approximately the first 3.5 months after launch are unchanged. Enquiries remain attributed to Frank. Concept websites remain labelled as concepts. Automation examples do not claim client results.

The hero animation, logo assets, navigation behaviour, form destination, privacy handling, and service/package values are preserved. CSS changes only accommodate package button labels and spacing in revised local-page copy.

## Verification

The Growth build and validator pass for 13 HTML pages and 11 publishable routes. All 13 routes were checked at widths of 320, 390, 768, and 1440 pixels. A narrow-phone case-study link and crowded tablet package buttons were corrected, then checked again at 320, 641, and 768 pixels.

Browser checks include service-aware enquiries, package selection, automation examples, and direct contact routes. No enquiry was submitted. A programmatic comparison confirmed preserved prices, terms, testimonial, numerical statistics, privacy body, and JavaScript behaviour outside the copy dictionary. These checks establish rendering and functional continuity, not conversion performance.
