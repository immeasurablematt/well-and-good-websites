AEO and AI Visibility

### Be part of the answer when a driver asks where to book service

Drivers can ask ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Google's AI answers where to take a vehicle instead of scrolling through links. AEO makes the shop's services, location, reputation, and business facts easier for those systems to find, understand, and cite. The business goal remains more service appointments and repair opportunities. No method guarantees a citation.

AEO, or answer engine optimization, is the practice of structuring your website and content so answer engines like ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Google's AI answers can find, understand, and cite your business when people ask questions. It relies on clear writing, direct answers, structured data, and consistent facts about your business across the web.

SEO helps your website rank in a list of search results. AEO helps your business appear inside an AI-generated answer, where a system summarizes a recommendation instead of showing links. They share a foundation of clear content and structured data, but AEO focuses on being quotable and verifiable, and it is measured by citations and mentions rather than ranking positions.

What the work covers

#### What AEO with Well and Good covers

#### Direct-answer content

Content written to answer real questions clearly and concisely, in the self-contained passages that answer engines prefer to quote.

#### Structured data and schema

Markup that tells machines what your business is, what it offers, and how its facts connect, so answer engines can parse it with less ambiguity.

#### Consistent entity signals

Consistent business facts, name, location, and services across your site and the wider web, so AI systems form a reliable picture of your business.

#### llms.txt and machine-readable context

A clear, accurate llms.txt and clean page structure that make your factual information easy for AI systems to read. This helps machines understand your business. It does not force any system to cite you.

#### Visibility monitoring

Checking how your business appears across Google, ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini over time, and recording citations, mentions, and recommendations separately so the picture is accurate.

Honesty about AEO

#### What AEO can and cannot promise

No one can guarantee that an AI system will cite your business, and Well and Good will never claim otherwise. Answer engines change their behaviour often, and they choose sources on their own terms. What AEO does is remove the technical and editorial reasons a system might overlook you, and give it clean, consistent, quotable information to work with. We measure what actually happens and report it plainly.

An llms.txt file gives AI systems a clear, structured summary of your business and its most useful pages. It makes your information easier to read. It is not a ranking mechanism, it is not required by any AI provider, and it does not guarantee that any system will use or cite it. Well and Good treats it as one signal among several, not a shortcut.

Questions and answers

#### AEO questions, answered

#### Can you guarantee ChatGPT or Claude will recommend my business?

No, and be cautious of anyone who says they can. AI systems choose their own sources and change often. AEO improves the odds by making your business clear, consistent, and easy to cite, then measures what actually happens over time.

#### How do you measure AI visibility?

By checking target questions across Google, ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini on a regular cadence, and recording citations, mentions, and recommendations separately. That separation keeps the reporting clear, because a mention is not the same as a recommendation.

#### Is AEO worth doing if I already do SEO?

For many businesses, yes, because they build on the same foundation. The clear content and structured data that help AEO also help SEO. The strategy call is the place to decide whether AI visibility is a priority for your market yet.

Questions and answers

#### Automation questions, answered

#### What is the difference between AI automation and traditional automation?

Traditional automation follows a fixed script and breaks when the input varies. An AI agent can handle variation and make small decisions within limits you set, which suits messy real-world tasks like intake and research. Both have their place, and some workflows are better served by simple rules than by an agent.

#### What should not be automated?

Anything where judgment, relationships, money, permissions, or irreversible actions carry real risk should keep a person in control. Well and Good designs for approval at those points rather than removing the human, because the goal is to save time without giving up oversight.

#### Do your agents have access to my accounts and money?

Only with your explicit permission, and only where it is genuinely needed. Sensitive actions, especially anything involving money or customer communication, are designed to pause for your approval rather than run on their own.

#### Can I start small?

Yes, and that is usually the right approach. One well-chosen workflow proves the value and builds trust before anything larger is attempted.
