# Contact

**Route:** `/contact/`  
**Primary CTA:** Send my request  
**Job:** Help visitors choose the right enquiry path and collect only the details needed for a useful response.

## SEO

**Title:** Contact Well & Good Growth  
**Meta description:** Request a free website prototype, discuss local growth, or describe a repetitive workflow for Well & Good Growth in Welland and Niagara.

## Copy

### Hero

**Eyebrow:** Contact  
# Tell us what needs to work better.

Choose the starting point that matches the problem. A website begins with a private prototype. Growth starts with the services and local searches you want more of. Automation starts with one repetitive workflow.

### Website prototype

Send your business name and a current Google, social, or website link. Well & Good Growth will use public services, reviews, business details, and publicly available photos where available to build a private website prototype.

**Best for:** A weak, missing, or unclear website that makes it harder for the right customer to choose you.

### Growth conversation

Tell us what services you want more of, the areas you serve, and what a useful enquiry looks like. The first conversation checks whether the website, local search foundation, conversion path, and tracking are ready for ongoing growth work.

**Best for:** A live business that wants to pursue more qualified enquiries, appointments, and sales beyond referrals alone.

### Automation conversation

Describe the repetitive task, the tools involved, how often it happens, and where you need a person to approve the work.

**Best for:** A process that takes real time, follows a pattern, or causes dropped balls when everyone is busy.

### Form copy

**Fields**

- Your name
- Business name
- Email or phone
- Current website, social link, or Google Business Profile
- What do you need help with? Website prototype / Growth / Automation
- What should customers do on your site, or what workflow do you want to improve?
- Anything else we should know?

**Button:** Send my request

**Form note:** Your details are used only to respond to your enquiry. See the [privacy notice](`/privacy/`).

### Final reassurance

You do not need a perfect brief. Send the useful details you have, and Well & Good Growth will reply with the right next step.

## Notes

- Website CTA language stays specific to a free website prototype.
- Automation needs are not forced into a prototype form.
- A technical implementation should route successful submissions to `/thank-you/`.
