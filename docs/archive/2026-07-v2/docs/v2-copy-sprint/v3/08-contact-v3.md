# Contact

**Route:** `/contact/`
**Primary CTA:** Send my request
**Job:** Honour the "send two things" promise made on every other page by asking for almost nothing, and route the enquiry correctly.

## SEO

**Title:** Contact Well & Good Growth
**Meta description:** Request a free website prototype, start a growth conversation, or describe a repetitive workflow. Four fields, no brief required.

## Copy

### Hero

**Eyebrow:** Contact
# Tell me what needs to work better.

A website starts with a private prototype. Growth starts with the work you want more of. Automation starts with one repetitive process. Pick whichever matches the problem and send the short version.

### Website prototype

Send your business name and a current Google, social, or website link. I build a private prototype from your public services, reviews, business details, and whatever photos are already out there, so you are looking at your own business rather than a template.

**Best for:** A weak, missing, or unclear website that makes it harder for the right customer to choose you.

### Growth conversation

Tell me what you want more of, where you work, and what counts as a useful enquiry. The first conversation checks whether the site, the local search foundation, the conversion path, and the tracking are actually ready for ongoing work.

**Best for:** A live business that wants steadier new work beyond referrals.

### Automation conversation

Describe the repetitive process, the tools involved, how often it happens, and where a person needs to approve the work. That first call is free.

**Best for:** A process that eats real time, follows a pattern, or drops balls when everyone is busy.

### Form copy

**Required fields, and that is the whole list**

- Your name
- Business name
- Email or phone
- Current website, social link, or Google Business Profile

**One choice**

- What do you need? Website prototype / Growth / Automation

**Optional, and genuinely optional**

- Anything you want me to know

**Button:** Send my request

**Support line under the button:** Four fields and a dropdown. That is enough to start.

**Form note:** Your details are only used to answer your enquiry. See the [privacy notice](`/privacy/`).

### After you send

You get a confirmation immediately, and a real reply from me with the right next step. If you asked for a prototype, I do not need anything else from you to start building it.

### Final reassurance

You do not need a brief, a budget, or a clear idea of what you want. Send what you have.

## Notes

- Rewritten from `08-contact.md`. This was the highest-value unfixed problem in the pack.
- The original form asked for seven fields, including "What should customers do on your site, or what workflow do you want to improve?" and "Anything else we should know?" Every other page in the pack promises the visitor only has to send two things. Asking for seven at the point of conversion breaks that promise exactly where it costs the most.
- Four required fields plus one dropdown. The open-ended questions that used to be required are now a single optional box, and the detail they were collecting is better gathered in the reply than demanded up front.
- The "what should customers do on your site" question is genuinely useful, and it belongs on the post-submission page or in the first reply. It is not a gate.
- Added an "After you send" section. A visitor who has just submitted a form wants to know what happens next, and the page previously ended without telling them.
- Automation's free-first-call term is stated here so it matches `04-automation-v3.md` and `05-how-it-works-v3.md`.
- Implementation should route successful submissions to `/thank-you/`.
