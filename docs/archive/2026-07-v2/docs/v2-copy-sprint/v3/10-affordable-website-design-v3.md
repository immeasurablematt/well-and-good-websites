### What people are really asking when they search for this

**Will it look cheap?**
You do not have to take a view on that. Send your business name and a Google or social link and I will build you a private prototype from your public details, free. Look at it, then decide. That is the only honest answer to this question and it is why the offer exists.

**Will I get nickel-and-dimed?**
A one-time build is a one-time build. You own it. Growth is a separate decision you can make later or never, and the ads arrangement, including the 12% management fee and the $250 monthly minimum, is written on the pricing page rather than discovered in an invoice.

**Can I change anything afterwards?**
Care at $39 per month covers hosting, SSL, backups, monitoring, and one minor edit a month, so a new phone number or a change of hours does not turn into a project. Anything bigger gets quoted before it starts.

### Affordable is not the same as disposable

Cheap websites usually cost more in the end, because they are built to be replaced. What you should be buying at this price is something narrow rather than something flimsy: fewer pages, done properly, that you own and can build on.

A one-page site is a strong fit when there is one main offer and one thing you want people to do. A multi-page site earns its cost when different services need their own explanation, when you cover several locations, or when different customers need different routes through.
