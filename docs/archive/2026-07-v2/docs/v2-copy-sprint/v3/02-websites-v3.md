### Questions worth answering

**Can a one-page website rank on Google?**
A focused page can compete for local searches when it carries clear services, real business details, structured data, and useful local signals. No page count and no build method guarantees a ranking, and anyone telling you otherwise is selling something.

**What do you need from me to start?**
Your business name and a current Google or social link. That is enough for a first prototype. Copy, proof, photos, services, and policies all get confirmed with you before anything goes live.

**What if I already have a website?**
Send the link. The prototype shows what a rebuild would look like, which is a more useful thing to argue about than an audit.
