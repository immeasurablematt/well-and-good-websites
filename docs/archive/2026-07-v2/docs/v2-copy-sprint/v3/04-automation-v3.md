# Take one repetitive job off your plate without handing over the keys.

Somebody in your business retypes the same information into a second system every week. Somebody chases the same follow-up. Somebody rebuilds the same report. It works, right up until the week everyone is busy and it quietly does not.

### A separate service line, on purpose

Automation has a different buyer, a different discovery process, different pricing, and a different proof base than Websites and Growth. It is not an upsell inside a website plan, and bundling it would only obscure what you are paying for.

The work begins by mapping one process: the steps, the tools already involved, the ways it currently goes wrong, and every point where a person needs to approve what happens next. Only then does a system get scoped.

What you should get out of it is time back, fewer dropped balls, and a process that no longer depends on someone remembering.

### What tends to be a fit

- Lead intake, sorting, and routing
- Recurring research and reporting
- Inbox and CRM preparation
- Structured document generation
- Meeting follow-up and action capture
- Knowledge organization and retrieval
- Monitoring and recurring internal checks

Some of these need nothing more than a rule and an integration. Others involve messy inputs where AI genuinely helps, inside defined limits. The system should match the work rather than force a fashionable tool onto it.

### The approval step is the point

Judgment, money, permissions, customer communication, and anything irreversible stay with a person. The system prepares the work, routes it, and then stops and waits.

That is not a hedge against the technology. It is the difference between automation you can leave running and automation you have to supervise, which is not automation at all.

### What the first conversation covers

A useful first call sorts out whether the process happens often enough to matter, takes real time, follows a recognizable pattern, and creates avoidable mistakes when it slips.

If the work is rare, or it depends on judgment at every single step, automating it is not worth your money. You should hear that on the first call rather than after a scoping fee.

That first call is free. If the process turns out to need deeper investigation before anything can be honestly scoped, that discovery becomes its own scoped paid piece of work, agreed in advance.
