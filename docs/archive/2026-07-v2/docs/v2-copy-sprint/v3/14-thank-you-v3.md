## Copy

### Confirmation

# Got it. Your request is in.

I read these myself and reply personally. You will hear back [VERIFY: within one business day].

### One thing that would help

You do not have to do this, and it does not hold anything up. But if you have two minutes, it makes what I send back noticeably better.

**If you asked for a website prototype**
Send photos. Your shop, your team, your trucks, your actual finished work. I build the prototype from what is already public, which usually means whatever ended up on Google. Real photos of your business beat that every time, and they beat stock images of somebody else's business by a mile.

Reply to the confirmation email with anything you have. Phone photos are fine.

**If you asked about growth**
Reply with the three jobs you want more of, and the one you would happily do less of. That single answer shapes the whole first conversation.

**If you asked about automation**
Reply with roughly how often the process runs and who currently touches it.

### What happens next

**Website prototype**
I go through your public services, reviews, business details, and photos, and build a private version of your site. You look at it and decide. No call required, and no obligation either way.

**Growth**
The conversation starts with what you want more of, where you work, the current site, and what counts as a useful enquiry.

**Automation**
The first call is free and covers the workflow, the tools involved, and where a person needs to stay in control. If it turns out not to be worth automating, I will tell you that.

## Notes

- Rewritten from `14-thank-you.md`.
- **Two changes worth arguing about, both deliberate.**
- First: the v2 note said "No response timeline is promised." That was the no-guarantees rule being applied to the wrong kind of claim. The rule exists to stop promises about rankings, citations, appointments, revenue, and timing of business outcomes, none of which are inside your control. When you reply to an email is entirely inside your control, and it is the single question every person who just submitted a form is asking. Both practitioner sources reviewed for this pack list a stated response expectation as a baseline convention. `[VERIFY]` marks the actual commitment: put in whatever you will genuinely hit every time, and if that is two business days, say two.
- Second: this page now makes a second ask. Someone who has just converted is the most willing they will ever be, and the photo request costs nothing while directly improving the prototype, which is the thing that closes the sale. It is framed as optional and explicitly does not gate anything.
- The photo ask also creates the difference the page can honestly claim: real business photos rather than stock, which is the one thing every practitioner source in this space agrees separates a credible local site from a template.
- Keep this route `noindex`.
- Kept free of pricing pressure. The links are informational and the CTA asks for photos, not money.
