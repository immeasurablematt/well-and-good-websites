### The experience behind it

I spent more than a decade producing measurable SEO, content, and social results through Jetta Grove Consulting, largely for B2B and technology brands. Search, content, and the operations work that keeps both running are not new to me.

**Label: Jetta Grove prior consultancy**
That decade is prior consultancy work. It is not Well & Good Growth client work, it was not local-business work, and it does not predict your result. I would rather say that plainly here than let it sit implied.

Well & Good Growth itself is new. Everything on the Work page is labelled for exactly what it is: what is live, what is a concept, what belongs to the prior consultancy, and what is an internal demonstration.

[See Work](`/work/`)

### Why I built this

Local businesses usually end up with a website from one vendor, scattered marketing from another, and a handful of processes held together by somebody remembering. Then nobody owns the result.

So the offer here is three clear things instead of one vague bundle. Build the website first. Grow the searches and conversion paths that matter once it is live. Or scope one repetitive workflow on its own terms. Three service lines, priced and sold separately, because bundling them mostly serves the seller.

### How I work

**Outcome first**
Start with the business result, then explain the mechanism in plain language. Nobody should have to nod along to jargon to buy a website.

**Published pricing**
Website prices are public so you can work out the budget before you speak to me. Growth and automation get quoted after a conversation, because they genuinely vary, and I will tell you what they depend on rather than going quiet.

**Human judgment stays put**
Automation should take routine work off your plate while a person still approves anything involving judgment, money, permissions, customer communication, or an action you cannot undo.

**No invented proof**
Live work, concepts, prior consultancy, and internal demonstrations are labelled separately and never merged into one impressive-looking pile.
