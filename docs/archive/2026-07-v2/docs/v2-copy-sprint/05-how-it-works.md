### Website path: understand, build, grow

**1. Send the basics**  
For a website, send your business name and a current Google, social, or website link. Well & Good Growth uses public details to prepare a private prototype.

**2. Approve the direction**  
Review the structure before you buy. Then confirm the services, proof, photos, policies, and customer action the live site needs.

**3. Launch the owned site**  
The site makes your services, reputation, location, expectations, and next step clear on a phone.

**4. Add growth when it is the right next move**  
Growth expands around the services and local searches you want to pursue, with search, profile, content, conversion, and tracking work chosen for the situation.

### Automation path: map first, build second

Automation starts with one repetitive workflow. Discovery maps the current process, the people and tools involved, permissions, failure cases, and every point where approval must stay with a person. Only then is a system scoped.

It may be a project or an ongoing system, depending on monitoring and maintenance needs. Its price is not tied to website packages.

[See Automation](`/services/automation/`)

### What determines the cost

Website pricing is published. Ongoing growth depends on the services, geography, competition, and publishing cadence required. Automation depends on the workflow steps, systems, permissions, failure risk, and oversight required.

### Questions worth answering

**Can I start with only a website?**  
Yes. One-time website builds are available, and growth is added only if it serves the next business goal.

**What is free before a purchase decision?**  
A website begins with a private prototype. An automation conversation begins with a free initial call. Detailed automation discovery may become scoped paid work when deeper investigation is needed.

**What if automation is not a fit?**  
If the work is too rare or depends on judgment at every step, it may not be worth automating. That is a useful answer.
