### When one page is enough

A focused page can be a good fit when the business has one primary offer, one location, and one action that matters most, such as calling, booking, requesting, ordering, or visiting.

It can include:

- A clear offer and who it is for
- Services or package details
- Reviews, photos, and business proof
- Hours, location, and contact details
- Questions customers ask before acting
- A call, booking, request, or contact path

### When you need more room

Choose a multi-page website when different services need their own explanations, several locations matter, the business needs deeper proof, or different customer groups need different paths. The goal is not to add pages for their own sake. It is to give the customer enough clarity to choose you.
