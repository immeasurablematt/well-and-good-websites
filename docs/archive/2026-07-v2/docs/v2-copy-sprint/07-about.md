### Why this practice exists

Local businesses often end up with a website from one vendor, scattered marketing activity from another, and manual processes held together by memory. Well & Good Growth brings a clearer choice: build the owned website first, grow the searches and conversion paths that matter, or scope one repetitive workflow separately.

Those are three distinct service lines, not one vague bundle.
