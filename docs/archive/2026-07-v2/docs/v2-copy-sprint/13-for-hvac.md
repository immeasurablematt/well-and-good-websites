### A website that earns the call or request

A strong HVAC website brings together the services you want to sell, the areas you serve, proof you are allowed to publish, and a clear call or request path. It should work on a phone and help a visitor understand whether they need repair, maintenance, installation, replacement, or a conversation first.

The capability: make your services, proof, and next step obvious before the customer contacts you.  
The benefit: more of the right local customers can choose you instead of a competitor with a clearer online presence.

### Build the pages around real customer decisions

Separate service paths can give each offer enough room to answer the questions that matter before a conversation. The final structure should be based on the company’s actual service mix, coverage area, seasonality, scheduling process, and policies.

Possible paths to confirm during discovery:

- Heating repair and maintenance
- Cooling repair and maintenance
- Installation or replacement
- Indoor air quality or other approved service lines
- Residential and light-commercial work, where relevant

No service, claim, licence, financing statement, or emergency availability statement should be published until the company confirms it.
