Well & Good Growth will review what you sent and respond personally.

### What happens next

**If you requested a website prototype**  
Your public services, reviews, business details, and publicly available photos where available will be reviewed to prepare a private direction for the site.

**If you asked about Growth**  
The next conversation will focus on the services you want more of, the areas you serve, the current website, and what counts as a useful customer action.

**If you asked about Automation**  
The next conversation will focus on the repetitive workflow, the tools involved, and where human approval needs to stay in place.
