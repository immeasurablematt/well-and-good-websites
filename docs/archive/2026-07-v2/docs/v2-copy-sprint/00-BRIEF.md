## Service-line matrix

### Websites
- Pain: customers search/compare; weak owned site loses the call
- Capability: make reputation, services, and next step obvious before contact
- Benefit: more of the right local customers choose you

### Growth
- Pain: site launches then sits while competitors keep winning searches
- Capability: turn more local searches into qualified enquiries, appointments, and sales
- Benefit: steadier appropriate new business beyond referrals alone

### Automation
- Pain: one repetitive workflow wastes hours
- Capability: remove that workflow while keeping human approval at risk points
- Benefit: time back, fewer dropped balls
- Separate arm: different buyer, discovery, pricing, proof
