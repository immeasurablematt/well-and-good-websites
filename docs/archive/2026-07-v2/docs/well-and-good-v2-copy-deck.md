## Section - What AEO is

**Heading:** What is AEO?
**Definition passage:** AEO, or answer engine optimization, is the practice of structuring your website and content so answer engines like ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Google's AI answers can find, understand, and cite your business when people ask questions. It relies on clear writing, direct answers, structured data, and consistent facts about your business across the web.

## Section - How AEO differs from SEO

**Heading:** How is AEO different from SEO?
**Definition passage:** SEO helps your website rank in a list of search results. AEO helps your business appear inside an AI-generated answer, where a system summarizes a recommendation instead of showing links. They share a foundation of clear content and structured data, but AEO focuses on being quotable and verifiable, and it is measured by citations and mentions rather than ranking positions.

## Section - What the work covers

**Heading:** What AEO with Well and Good covers

**Item 1 - Direct-answer content**
Content written to answer real questions clearly and concisely, in the self-contained passages that answer engines prefer to quote.

**Item 2 - Structured data and schema**
Markup that tells machines what your business is, what it offers, and how its facts connect, so answer engines can parse it with less ambiguity.

**Item 3 - Consistent entity signals**
Consistent business facts, name, location, and services across your site and the wider web, so AI systems form a reliable picture of your business.

**Item 4 - llms.txt and machine-readable context**
A clear, accurate `llms.txt` and clean page structure that make your factual information easy for AI systems to read. This helps machines understand your business. It does not force any system to cite you.

**Item 5 - Visibility monitoring**
Checking how your business appears across Google, ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini over time, and recording citations, mentions, and recommendations separately so the picture is honest.

## Section - Honesty about AEO

**Heading:** What AEO can and cannot promise
**Body:** No one can guarantee that an AI system will cite your business, and Well and Good will never claim otherwise. Answer engines change their behaviour often, and they choose sources on their own terms. What AEO does is remove the technical and editorial reasons a system might overlook you, and give it clean, consistent, quotable information to work with. We measure what actually happens and report it plainly.

## Section - llms.txt honesty

**Heading:** What llms.txt does and does not do
**Body:** An `llms.txt` file gives AI systems a clear, structured summary of your business and its most useful pages. It makes your information easier to read. It is not a ranking mechanism, it is not required by any AI provider, and it does not guarantee that any system will use or cite it. Well and Good treats it as one honest signal among several, not a shortcut.

## FAQ

**Q: Can you guarantee ChatGPT or Claude will recommend my business?**
A: No, and be cautious of anyone who says they can. AI systems choose their own sources and change often. AEO improves the odds by making your business clear, consistent, and easy to cite, then measures what actually happens over time.

**Q: How do you measure AI visibility?**
A: By checking target questions across Google, ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini on a regular cadence, and recording citations, mentions, and recommendations separately. That separation keeps the reporting honest, because a mention is not the same as a recommendation.

**Q: Is AEO worth doing if I already do SEO?**
A: For many businesses, yes, because they build on the same foundation. The clear content and structured data that help AEO also help SEO. The strategy call is the place to decide whether AI visibility is a priority for your market yet.
