Three directions for Well and Good Growth

All three keep your name, your approved copy, and the Welland lift bridge. They differ in how the bridge is drawn, the type, and how things move.

Each one shows the new mark, the homepage, and the AI automation page with a moving drawing of a follow-up workflow. The logo uses a simpler bridge than the illustrations so it stays clear at small sizes, like a browser tab.

This is a preview only. Your website has not changed. The drawings are quick studies, and the one you pick gets refined.

A

Ink line

Fine ink lines that draw themselves on warm paper. Fraunces stays, set lighter, so this is the closest to your current site.

%%SHEET_A%%

Why pick it

It is exactly the mood you chose, and the calmest of the three. It is also the easiest to roll out, because it keeps your current typeface.

Watch out for

It is the smallest change from the site you have now, so some visitors may not notice a rebrand at all.

B

Canal lock

A two-colour print look: forest and coral inks on sand paper, heavier lines, and a sturdy serif. The automation drawing is a boat rising through canal locks.

%%SHEET_B%%

Why pick it

It is the most memorable, and it is rooted in Welland. The lock drawing shows your review step in one picture: the job only rises once you open the gate.

Watch out for

It is the biggest change, and the printed look leans a little retro. The lock idea needs its caption to land the first time.

C

Draftsman

An engineer's drawing: grid paper, construction lines, and numbered callouts. The review step is a hold point, the term builders use for where work stops until it is checked.

%%SHEET_C%%

Why pick it

It signals care and precision, which suits the automation work, and the hold point is a clear way to explain review.

Watch out for

It is the coolest of the three. It could read as an engineering firm rather than a local partner.

My pick: B, Canal lock

It is the most distinct from other agencies and from your current site, and the lock drawing turns your promise of clear review points into a picture people remember. If it feels too heavy, the fine lines of A with the lock drawing from B would work well.
