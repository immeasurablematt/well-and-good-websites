Questions about pricing and delivery

What does the setup or onboarding fee pay for?

Discovery, research, account access, website planning and build work, analytics and tracking, Google Business Profile and search setup, and the initial systems required by your plan.

Is setup charged with annual billing?

Yes. Setup or onboarding is charged once and is additional to either monthly or annual recurring billing. Annual billing covers twelve months of service and includes approximately two months free.

What is included in a free preview?

A personalized homepage concept or focused website preview based on your business. It shows the proposed direction, structure, and primary message. It is not a completed free website.

What counts as a small edit?

A text, photo, hours, price, or similar change to an existing section that generally takes 30 minutes or less. New pages, sections, redesigns, integrations, and functionality are scoped separately.

Do unused deliverables roll over?

No. Monthly edits, posts, content, and other recurring deliverables reset each month so the workload and publishing schedule stay predictable.

Who supplies video footage?

The client supplies footage for the two edited Market Leader videos. On-site photography or filming is not included and can be quoted separately.

Are backlinks guaranteed?

No. Market Leader includes qualified local and industry outreach. Third-party sites decide what they publish, so acquired links cannot be promised.

Is advertising spend included?

No. Paid advertising spend is separate. Campaign management, professional photography, filming, e-commerce, and custom applications are quoted separately.

What happens to the website if I cancel?

You keep your domain and business content. Once outstanding invoices are paid, a portable copy of the website can be provided. Hosting, maintenance, licensed tools, and recurring marketing stop at the end of the paid term unless you choose Care or arrange other hosting.

When does the one-business-day preview clock begin?

After payment and all required business information, account access, brand assets, photos, and approvals needed to start have been received.

Can a one-time website be upgraded?

Yes. A one-time build can move into Foundation, Growth, or Market Leader. The onboarding scope and fee will reflect what is already complete and what still needs to be set up.

What can delay publishing?

Client approval delays may move publishing dates. The client must supply required account access, assets, accurate business information, and approvals. Domain access, revisions, and third-party systems can also affect the final launch date.
